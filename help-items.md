# Murder‑Mystery‑Bot — Items Guide

Last updated: December 23, 2025

Below is a list of all items. While in‑game, you can show this list during night time with `!shop`. Items are bought at night with `!buy <item>` and used with `!use <item> [optional argument]`.

Some items have spaces in their names; the identifier to type when buying or using is shown in square brackets `[]`.

### Rock :rock:
- Effect: 30% chance to kill the specified player
- Cost: :coin: 3
- Usage: `!use rock @<player>`
- Buy: `!buy rock`

### Gun :real_gun:
- Effect: Kills the specified player
- Cost: :coin: 7
- Usage: `!use gun @<player>`
- Buy: `!buy gun`

### Ring Of Life [ring] :ring:
- Effect: Unless executed, you will not die when you get killed
- Cost: :coin: 15
- Usage: Activates automatically
- Buy: `!buy ring`

### Shield :shield:
- Effect: Unless executed, 50% chance you won't die when killed
- Cost: :coin: 8
- Usage: Activates automatically
- Buy: `!buy shield`

### Potato :potato:
- Effect: Unless executed, 10% chance you won't die when killed
- Cost: :coin: 3
- Usage: Activates automatically
- Buy: `!buy potato`

### Lottery Ticket [ticket] :tickets:
- Effect: Small chance to win the lottery the next morning (grand prize 30 gold; smaller prizes possible). You can only participate in one lottery per day; extra tickets roll to the next day.
- Cost: :coin: 4
- Usage: Activates automatically
- Buy: `!buy ticket`

### House :house:
- Effect: 100% chance to survive a tornado; 30% chance the house breaks afterward
- Cost: :coin: 4
- Usage: Activates automatically
- Buy: `!buy house`

### Dagger :dagger:
- Effect: Kill someone at night without publicly revealing who did it
- Cost: :coin: 10
- Usage: `!use dagger @<player>`
- Buy: `!buy dagger`

### Phone :telephone:
- Effect: Reply to the last broadcast
- Cost: :coin: 6
- Usage: `!use phone <text>`
- Buy: `!buy phone`

### Satellite :satellite:
- Effect: Send broadcasts while pretending to be the broadcaster
- Cost: :coin: 4
- Usage: `!use satellite`
- Buy: `!buy satellite`

### Bandage :adhesive_bandage:
- Effect: If you're the doctor, regain one use of your ability (if already used)
- Restriction: Only purchasable by the :pill: doctor
- Cost: :coin: 6
- Usage: `!use bandage`
- Buy: `!buy bandage`
