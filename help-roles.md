# Murder‑Mystery‑Bot — Roles Guide

Last updated: December 23, 2025

This tutorial lists all roles and their abilities. Some roles are always present; others appear only when enough players have joined the game.

## Always Included

### Murderer :dagger:
- Ability: Kill one player every night.
- Goal: Kill everyone.
- Availability: Always in every game.

### Doctor :pill:
- Ability: When the murderer kills someone, the doctor is notified and can choose to heal them (only once per game).
- Goal: Kill the murderer.
- Availability: Always in every game.

## Conditional Roles
(Shown when the minimum player requirement is met.)

### Detective :spy:
- Ability: Inspect a player's role at night; the result is revealed the next night.
- Goal: Kill the murderer.
- Minimum players: 4

### Banker :person_in_tuxedo:
- Ability: Starts with 3 gold and receives +1 extra gold per day.
- Goal: Kill the murderer.
- Minimum players: 4

### Thief :unlock:
- Ability: Each night, attempt to steal :coin: gold from someone. 50% chance to steal half of that player's gold.
- Goal: Kill the murderer.
- Minimum players: 4

### Jailer :cop:
- Ability: Choose someone to jail each night; they will be jailed the next night. Jailed players cannot use the shop or their role's ability.
- Goal: Kill the murderer.
- Minimum players: 5

### Broadcaster :radio:
- Ability: Send a message to players of a specific role at night without knowing who they are, or broadcast to everyone at night. Messages are received the following night.
- Goal: Kill the murderer.
- Minimum players: 6

### Fool :clown:
- Ability/Win Condition: If the fool is voted for execution during the day, they win; if killed in any other way, they lose.
- Goal: Get voted for execution.
- Minimum players: 6

### Hunter :hunter:
- Ability: Shoot someone at night. If the target is not the :dagger: murderer or the :wolf: werewolf, the hunter dies and the target survives.
- Goal: Kill the murderer.
- Minimum players: 6

### Werewolf :wolf:
- Ability: Works together with the murderer. On nights with full moon, the werewolf must kill someone.
- Goal: Kill everyone except the murderer.
- Minimum players: 7

### Cupid :bow_and_heart:
- Ability: Select two players (including self if desired) to make them fall in love. Lovers share fate: if one dies, the other dies too. Lovers can talk during night. If one lover is the :dagger: murderer, their goal changes to kill everyone except their lover.
- Goal: Kill the murderer (unless aligned with murderer as a lover).
- Minimum players: 8
