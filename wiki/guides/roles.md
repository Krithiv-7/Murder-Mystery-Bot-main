# Roles

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Some roles are in every game. Others appear only when enough players have joined. Night choices arrive by DM as a menu. The same list is available in Discord with `!wiki` and `/wiki`.

## Always included

### Murderer

Kills one player every night. Wins by being the last side standing. Always in the game.

### Doctor

When the murderer or werewolf attacks someone, the doctor gets a DM with **Heal them** and **Let them die**. The heal can be used only once per game, including if the doctor is the target. Wins by killing the murderer. Always in the game.

## Conditional roles

### Detective

Inspects a player's role at night. The result arrives the next night. Minimum players: 4.

### Banker

Starts with 3 gold and receives one extra gold per day. Minimum players: 4.

### Thief

Each night, may steal half of someone's gold. The chance is 50%. Minimum players: 4.

### Mayor

A daytime vote counts as two votes. Minimum players: 4.

### Jailer

Jails someone for the next night. A jailed player cannot use their ability or the shop. Minimum players: 5.

### Bodyguard

Protects one player each night. If that player is attacked, the bodyguard dies instead. Minimum players: 5.

### Broadcaster

Sends a message to one role, or to everyone, without learning who has that role. The message arrives the following night. Minimum players: 6.

### Fool

Wins if the town votes to execute them. Loses if they die any other way. Minimum players: 6.

### Hunter

Shoots someone at night. If the target is not the murderer or the werewolf, the hunter dies and the target lives. Minimum players: 6.

### Medium

Once per game, receives a DM naming the role of one dead player. Minimum players: 6.

### Werewolf

Works with the murderer. On a full moon the werewolf must kill someone. Wins with the murderer. Minimum players: 7.

### Cupid

Chooses two players, and may include themselves. If one lover dies, the other dies. Lovers get private DMs. If a lover is the murderer, that murderer's goal becomes killing everyone except their lover. Minimum players: 8.
