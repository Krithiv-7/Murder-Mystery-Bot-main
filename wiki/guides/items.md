# Items

Last updated: September 29, 2026

During the night, use `!shop` and `!buy <item>`, or `/shop` and `/buy`, from your DM. Use an item with `!use` or `/use`. If a name has a space, buy and use the id in brackets.

### Rock

30% chance to kill the chosen player. Cost: 3 gold. `!use rock @player`.

### Gun

Kills the chosen player. Cost: 7 gold. `!use gun @player`.

### Ring of life `[ring]`

Unless you are executed, you survive a kill. Cost: 15 gold. Activates on its own. `!buy ring`.

### Shield

Unless you are executed, 50% chance to survive a kill. Cost: 8 gold. Activates on its own.

### Potato

Unless you are executed, 10% chance to survive a kill. Cost: 3 gold. Activates on its own.

### Lottery ticket `[ticket]`

A small chance to win gold the next morning. The grand prize is 30 gold. Only one ticket is drawn per day; extras wait until the next day. Cost: 4 gold. `!buy ticket`.

### House

You survive a tornado. There is a 30% chance the house breaks afterward. Cost: 4 gold. Activates on its own.

### Dagger

Kills someone at night without announcing who did it. Cost: 10 gold. `!use dagger @player`.

### Phone

Reply to the last broadcast. Cost: 6 gold. `!use phone <text>`.

### Satellite

Send broadcasts while pretending to be the broadcaster. Cost: 4 gold. `!use satellite`.

### Bandage

If you are the doctor and have already healed someone, this restores one heal. Only the doctor can buy it. Cost: 6 gold. `!use bandage`.
